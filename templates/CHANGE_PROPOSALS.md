# Instruction-change proposals

No proposals or learner decisions are recorded in this blank starter.
Pending proposals are not active instructions.

For each actual proposal record:

- ID and status: proposed / approved / rejected / applied / verified.
- Observation and readable evidence, separate from a suspected cause.
- Existing rule overlap and whether data, access, tooling, code, or checks are the cause.
- Exact before/after text, file, scope, and conflicts.
- Test, success criterion, and a new counterexample.
- The learner's explicit decision, approved scope, and date.
- Application and verification evidence, if authorized.

Keep project-specific rules local. Generalization requires its own evidence and
approval. Independent teaching can continue while a proposal remains pending.
